## Direction

Signal Desk treats JobHunter as a professional decision instrument. Its visual center is the user's match score and the evidence behind it. The product should feel energetic while discovering work, measured while tracking applications, and quiet while editing a profile.

## Durable Rules

- Cobalt is reserved for commands, current navigation, focus, and active progress. It is not ambient decoration.
- Match score always uses the four semantic tiers defined in `DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`; color is accompanied by a number and label.
- Information density follows the task: dense Marketplace, measured Tracking, quiet Profile and AI configuration.
- Surfaces are separated by contrast, fine borders, and directional shadows. Avoid floating layers without hierarchy.
- Instrument Sans carries interface text. Tabular system monospace is limited to scores, measurements, dates, and technical identifiers.
- Corners remain controlled: 10px controls, 14px cards, 18px major panels. Pills are reserved for tags and statuses.
- Motion communicates loading, state changes, route changes, and drag/drop. All content remains usable with reduced motion.
- Dark mode is a first-class palette, not an inversion. Cobalt and status colors become lighter to preserve contrast on navy surfaces.

## Anti-Patterns

- Generic pastel job-board cards, decorative gradients, glass panels, and ornamental blur.
- Match scores reduced to an undifferentiated badge.
- Emoji as product iconography.
- Hidden errors, empty tables without guidance, and animation that delays an action.
- Claims of guaranteed employment outcomes or infallible ATS optimization.
